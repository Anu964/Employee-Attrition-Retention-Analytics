# Employee Attrition & Retention Analytics

An end-to-end HR Analytics project focused on understanding employee attrition and retention patterns using **Excel, MySQL, Python, and Power BI**.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Employee attrition is an important HR challenge because frequent employee turnover can affect workforce stability, productivity, and hiring costs.

This project analyzes employee data to identify patterns associated with attrition and retention across different workforce factors such as department, job role, overtime, salary, job satisfaction, work-life balance, promotion gap, tenure, and performance.

The analysis was performed using **Excel, MySQL, Python, and Power BI**, with the final results presented through an interactive three-page Power BI dashboard.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The main objectives of this project are to:

- Measure overall employee attrition and retention
- Identify departments and job roles with different attrition patterns
- Analyze the relationship between overtime and attrition
- Examine salary ranges and employee attrition
- Analyze job satisfaction and work-life balance
- Study tenure and promotion gaps in relation to attrition
- Understand employee retention patterns
- Present HR insights through an interactive dashboard

## 📊 Dataset

The project uses the **IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition & Performance** dataset.

The dataset contains employee-level information such as:

- Age
- Attrition
- Department
- Job Role
- Monthly Income
- Overtime
- Job Satisfaction
- Work-Life Balance
- Years at Company
- Years Since Last Promotion
- Performance Rating
- Business Travel
- Education
- Gender

### Dataset Size

- **1,470 employees**
- **33 columns** after data preparation
